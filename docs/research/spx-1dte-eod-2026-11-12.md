# SPXW end-of-day 1-DTE study — November 12, 2026

Decision: **no qualifying profitable policy under the modeled execution costs**.
Reject the tested complete settlement policies for a one-fly-per-entry deployment;
the incomplete timed-exit policies do not establish an edge. This is not a claim
that every possible overnight butterfly strategy is unprofitable.

The owner requested buying a butterfly near the close for the next session's
expiration, comparing direction and strike placement. The standalone report is
[`index.html`](../../reports/research/spx-1dte-eod-20261112/index.html). Private
derived evidence lives alongside it; original vendor quotes remain local.

## Frozen experiment

- Genuine SPXW next-session-expiry minute NBBO observations; fixed CALL
  construction, +1 lower / -2 body / +1 upper. No synthetic option prices.
- Decision at 15:45 ET, exact 15:46 entry, excluding scheduled early-close entry
  dates. Expiration must be the next session in the attributed Cboe daily calendar.
- Neutral placement; continuation or reversal of the 09:35–15:45 or
  14:45–15:45 move. Signals use a same-expiry zero-carry put/call parity forward
  proxy, not observed index spot. Exactly flat directional signals are excluded.
- Directional body offsets 0.5 and 1.0 times observed ATM-straddle premium points;
  these are not measured sigma. Widths 20 and 40 points. Nearest five-point body,
  lower on ties. Eighteen entries, with 09:45, noon, or settlement exits: 54 policies.
- Missing scheduled exits remain unresolved. No settlement fallback is silently
  imposed on the timed policies. Settlement uses the attributed Cboe SPX close.
- Opening component debit uses wing asks minus twice the body bid; closing
  credit uses wing bids minus twice body ask, retaining signed negative values.
  Transaction-needed displayed sizes cover one wing contract and two body
  contracts. Component quotes are modeled cash flows, not actual complex fills.
- Assumed commission $0.65 per option contract per transaction. Opening a fly
  costs $2.60; a traded exit costs another $2.60. Cash settlement has no closing
  transaction fee in this study. Additional adverse stress is $0.05 **per whole
  fly** per transaction ($5), with $0.20 sensitivity reported separately.

Entry-date windows: development 2023-01-03–2024-06-28; temporal comparison
2026-03-13–2026-09-25. Protected entry **or expiration** dates in
2024-07-01–2026-03-12 are excluded before quote access. These historical windows
have prior research exposure; the later segment is not an untouched holdout.

The selection rule was frozen before returns: highest development stressed
**dollar** expectancy among policies with at least 100 completed entries and at
least 95% completion, then lexical tie-break. It selected
`day_continue|0.5|40|settlement` before comparison quotes were replayed.

## Findings from corrected run-02

The selected policy completed all 362 accepted development entries, losing
$3,363.20, or $9.29 per fly (49.17% wins; profit factor 0.9845). It completed all
129 accepted later-period entries, losing $30,088.40, or $233.24 per fly
(31.78% wins; profit factor 0.6612).

All 18 settlement policies had negative development dollar expectancy. Nine
timed policies had positive development averages on their priced subsets, but
none satisfied the completion screen. All 54 later-period priced-subset means
were negative. Missing timed exits preclude complete-cohort profitability
claims for those policies.

For the selected policy, midpoint-after-fee development expectancy was +$69.25,
versus -$4.29 at component prices before the extra $5 stress. Its later-period
midpoint expectancy was already -$155.78. On matched neutral-baseline dates,
placement improved development expectancy by $41.22 (357 pairs) but worsened
later expectancy by $28.45 (123 pairs). Midpoints do not establish attainable
complex-order prices.

The study models independent one-lot episodes, with varying debit risk and
possible overlap near the close for settlement positions. It defines no funded
portfolio return. Drawdown uses terminal outcomes and excludes intraday and
overnight marks. Equal-weight mean R can differ in sign from dollar expectancy
because trade debits vary; no risk-normalized sizing strategy was tested.
