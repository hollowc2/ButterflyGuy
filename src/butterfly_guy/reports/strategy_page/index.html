Strategy explained · 0-DTE __UNDERLYING__

Pay a dime.
Aim for the pin.

Every morning Butterfly Guy buys one out-of-the-money __UNDERLYING__ butterfly that expires the same afternoon, parked in the direction the market gapped. It costs about a tenth of its width. If __UNDERLYING__ settles near the middle strike it pays ~__RR_TARGET__× what it cost. Most days it doesn't — the few that do carry the rest.

Prices are modeled (Black-Scholes, time left from the entry window to the close, flat vol scaled from the VIX) to show the mechanics — not live quotes, and real skew shifts them. The selection mirrors the bot: center within ± pts of the VIX target, cost under the per-width cap, reward:risk of at least __RR_MIN__:1, closest to __RR_TARGET__:1, wider wing on ties.

How a day runs

  1. 01 · open

    Read the gap

    Compare __UNDERLYING__ with yesterday's close. Gapped up: buy a call fly above the market. Gapped down: a put fly below it.

  2. 02 · size

    Let the VIX set the tent

    The VIX regime picks three wing widths. Each gets a center __SIGMAS__ of the VIX-implied daily move away from spot.

  3. 03 · __ENTRY_WINDOW__

    Only buy it cheap

    Take the fly closest to __RR_TARGET__:1 that costs at most __MAX_COST_PCT__ of its width. __PER_DAY__, __CONTRACTS__. Worst case: lose the debit.

  4. 04 · manage

    Trail the peak

    Once it's been green, exit if its value falls this far below its high. Otherwise it rides to the 4:00 settle.
